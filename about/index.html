A small studio, built to stay small.

Red Rock Apps LLC is an independent studio based in the foothills of the Rocky Mountains, Colorado.

We design and build mobile applications for consumers, with a preference for tools that do one thing well over ones that try to do everything. One person, one app at a time, no outside funding and no plans to grow into something that needs it.

The name is a nod to Red Rocks Amphitheatre — the sandstone landmark just down the road, carved by the same geology that shapes the foothills around us.

How we build

Software that is paid for by the people who use it doesn't need to collect anything about them, so Screen Night doesn't. No analytics, no advertising SDK, no third-party tracking. One subscription, everything included, nothing held back for a higher tier.

The same thinking shapes what we leave out. No public profiles, no follower counts, no feed designed to be scrolled. A TV tracker should tell you what's on and then get out of the way.
