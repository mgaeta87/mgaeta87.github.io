How Screen Night compares with Trakt, Serializd, Sofa Time and JustWatch

Checked 1 October 2026 · US App Store prices

Who Screen Night is for

Screen Night is for people who watch TV with specific people — a partner, family, a few friends — and want to keep up together without a public profile, ads or tracking.

It isn't the right app if you want to track movies, want something free, use Android, or want a public community to talk about episodes. The apps below do those things well, and we say which one fits.

Which TV tracker fits you

  • Screen Night — you watch with people you know and want to see what they're watching and loved, with alerts that tell you the time where you live. No ads, no public profile.
  • Trakt — you want a big community, movies and TV together, and apps on every platform, and don't mind paying for VIP to lift the limits.
  • Serializd — you want a TV-only app like ours but free, and enjoy public reviews and discussions, with ads.
  • Sofa Time — you want a free app with a one-time upgrade, movies, a community feed and Android.
  • JustWatch — finding where to stream something matters most, and ads don't bother you.

Why Screen Night costs money

Screen Night is paid for by the people who use it, so it has no ads and no reason to track you. One price includes everything, with no limits on shows or lists.
