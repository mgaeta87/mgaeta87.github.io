02 — Screen Night

A calmer home for your watchlist.

Screen Night is an app for tracking TV shows: what's next, what you've finished, what's worth starting, and what your friends are watching, if you add them. No gamification, no ads.

See how it compares with other TV trackers →

What Screen Night does

  • Up Next, in air-date order. Every show you follow, sorted by when its next episode airs, with what you're in the middle of kept apart from what you mean to start.
  • Catch up fast. Mark a whole season watched in one tap, or use Mark Watched Through Season X for a show you're partway through.
  • Episode alerts. Optional alerts say when and where each new episode airs, at your local time, and let you mark it watched from the notification. They're scheduled on your iPhone.
  • Friends you choose. Connect by invite link, or find each other when you both have the other's number saved. See what they're watching, how far along they are and what they loved. There are no public profiles, followers or comments.
  • Simple ratings. Thumbs down, thumbs up or love it.
  • Your year in TV. Episodes, shows and hours watched, and the networks and streamers behind them.

Coming from TV Time? Screen Night doesn't import TV Time history. Add your shows and use Mark Watched Through Season X to set where you are.
