Screen Night is a place to start again — and a reminder of where you left off in everything you're part-way through.

Everything you're watching, in the order it airs.

Up Next puts every show you follow in one list, sorted by when its next episode actually airs — with what you're part-way through kept separate from what you mean to start.

  • Catch up in one tap. Mark a whole season watched, or everything through season 4 of a show you're behind on.
  • Alerts that know your time zone. Told when and where each episode airs, local to you — and you can mark it watched straight from the notification.
  • Works on the plane. Your library is on the device, not just the server.

The people you actually watch with. Nobody else.

Connect by invite link or a saved phone number, and see what your friends are watching, how far along they are, and what they loved. That's the whole social feature.

  • No public profiles, no followers, no comment sections.
  • Both sides opt in. A contact match only becomes a friend after a request and an accept.
  • Recommend a show with a link, and it opens right on their copy of the app.

Something worth starting next

Picks built from what you've actually rated and watched — not from what's trending everywhere. Shows your friends loved sit at the top.

Your year in TV, counted up

Episodes, shows and hours watched, your top five, and the networks and streamers behind them — ready to share when December comes around.

No analytics. No advertising. No tracking.

Screen Night is paid for by the people who use it, which is the whole reason there's nothing to sell about you. Your watch history is yours — exportable as CSV any time you want it, and gone for good when you delete your account.

Questions

Can I bring my history over from TV Time?
Not automatically — TV Time's export is gone. Add your shows, then use Mark Watched Through Season X to set where you are in each one. It takes a few minutes, not an evening.
Does it track movies?
No. Screen Night is TV only, deliberately. If you want both in one app, the comparison page says which one to use instead.
Will my friends see what I'm watching?
Only the people you've connected with by invite link or a mutual contact match, and only after you both agree. There are no public profiles, no followers, and you can pause sharing at any time.
Can I get my data out?
Yes. Settings → Your Data exports your watchlist, watched episodes and ratings as CSV files you can open in Numbers or Excel.
Does it work on iPad?
Yes — iPhone and iPad, one subscription, iOS 17 or later.
