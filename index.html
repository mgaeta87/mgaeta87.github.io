01 — About

A small studio, built to stay small.

Red Rock Apps LLC is an independent studio based in the foothills of the Rocky Mountains, Colorado. We design and build mobile applications for consumers, with a preference for tools that do one thing well over ones that try to do everything.

The name is a nod to Red Rocks Amphitheatre — the sandstone landmark just down the road, carved by the same geology that shapes the foothills around us.
