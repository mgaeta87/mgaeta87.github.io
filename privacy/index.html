Caught Up — iOS

Privacy Policy

Caught Up keeps track of what you watch. To do that across your devices it stores a small amount of data about you — and nothing beyond that. This page says exactly what, where it goes, and how to get rid of it.

Effective 25 August 2026

01 — The short version

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking.

Caught Up contains no analytics service, no advertising SDK, no crash-reporting service, and no third-party tracking of any kind. It does not use the device advertising identifier (IDFA) and never asks to track you across other apps or websites. It does not collect your location or your contacts.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data. There is no advertising in the app, so there is no one to share it with.

What we do store is the account you sign in with, and the shows and episodes you track — because that is the product.

02 — What we collect

Your account, and what you're watching.

Account information

When you sign up with an email address, we store that address and a securely hashed form of your password. We never store or see your password in readable form. If you sign in with Google or Facebook instead, we receive your email address and basic profile information from them, and never see your password for those services at all.

What you track

When you add a show, mark an episode watched, or rate something, we store that so it survives a reinstall and follows you to another device. In practice that is a small set of numeric identifiers — not free text, notes, or anything you write:

StoredWhat it contains
WatchlistThe numeric ID of each show you add.
Watched episodesThe show and episode IDs, the season and episode number, the date you marked it, and the show's genre labels.
RatingsThe show ID, your thumbs rating, and the show's genre labels.

Genre labels are stored alongside your ratings and watch history so the app can suggest shows without having to look every title up again. They describe the show, not you.

Notifications

Episode reminders and the weekly digest are scheduled entirely on your device. Nothing about what you watch is sent anywhere to produce them, and Caught Up uses no push notification service.

Diagnostic logs

When something goes wrong, the app writes technical detail to the device's own system log to help with troubleshooting. That stays on your device and is not transmitted to us.

03 — Where it goes

The services involved, and what each one sees.

Caught Up is a small app and relies on a small number of outside services. Each one receives only what it needs to do its job:

  • Supabase hosts the account system and the database holding your watchlist, watched episodes, and ratings. Access rules are enforced per row, so your data is readable only by your own account.
  • Apple handles all subscription payments. We never see or receive your card details. So that a subscription stays attached to the right Caught Up account, each purchase is tagged with a random internal account identifier — a meaningless string of characters that contains no personal information.
  • Resend delivers account emails, such as your confirmation code or a password reset. It receives your email address for that purpose.
  • Google and Facebook are involved only if you choose to sign in with one of them.
  • TVmaze supplies show and episode information. The app requests that data directly, so TVmaze's servers see the request and your device's IP address. No account information is attached to those requests, and TVmaze is never told who is asking.

Your data is stored on servers in the United States. If you are using Caught Up from elsewhere, that means your information is transferred to and processed there.

04 — Your choices

Getting your data, or getting rid of it.

You can delete your account at any time from inside the app — Settings → Delete Account. Deletion removes your account record along with your watchlist, watched episodes, and ratings, and it can't be undone. If you'd rather not use the app to do it, email mike@redrockappsllc.com from the address on the account and we'll delete it for you.

Deleting your account doesn't cancel an active subscription — that's managed separately through your Apple ID, and the app will tell you to cancel it first if one is active.

You may also ask us for a copy of the data we hold about you, or ask us to correct it. Depending on where you live — including the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and California — you may have additional rights to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data, and to object to how it is used. We honour these requests regardless of where you live. Write to the address above and we will respond.

05 — Retention

We keep it while you use the app.

Your account and tracking data are kept for as long as your account exists. When an account is deleted, the associated records are removed at the same time. Backups held by our hosting provider may retain copies for a short period afterwards before they expire.

06 — Children

Not directed to children.

Caught Up is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us their information, write to us and we will delete it.

07 — Changes

If this changes, it changes here.

When this policy is updated, the effective date at the top of the page changes with it. If a change materially affects how your information is handled, we will say so in the app rather than relying on you to check.
