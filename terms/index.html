The rules for using Screen Night, in plain language. The most important part is short: be decent to the people you connect with, or you won’t be able to keep using Friends.

One person, looked after.

03 — Subscriptions

Paid through Apple, cancelled through Apple.

Screen Night Premium is an auto-renewing subscription, monthly or yearly, bought through the App Store and billed to your Apple ID. It renews automatically at the price shown when you subscribed unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. A free trial, when offered, becomes a paid subscription at the end of the trial unless you cancel before then.

You manage and cancel your subscription in your Apple ID settings, not in Screen Night. Refunds are handled by Apple under its own policies. Deleting your Screen Night account does not cancel a subscription, which is why the app asks you to cancel first.

Promo codes unlock Screen Night for the account that redeems them. Each can be used once and has no cash value.

04 — Friends

No tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users.

Friends lets you connect with people you know and see what each other is watching. It only works if people can trust it, so when you use Friends you agree not to:

  • use a name, or share anything, that is hateful, harassing, sexually explicit, violent, threatening, or otherwise objectionable;
  • pretend to be someone else, including by entering a phone number that isn’t yours;
  • harass, pester or spam anyone, including with repeated friend requests or recommendations;
  • try to find out anyone’s phone number or identity through Screen Night, or get around a block.

You can block anyone. Blocking removes the connection both ways, stops their requests and notifications, and hides you from each other. They are not told.

You can report anyone. Reports come to us, and the person you report isn’t told who reported them. We review every report within 24 hours. If someone has broken these rules we remove what they posted, and we may suspend or delete their account, without warning. Names that break these rules may also be refused when they’re entered.

05 — What you share

Yours, shown to the friends you choose.

When you connect with someone, they can see your name and avatar, the shows you’re watching and how far along you are, the shows you’ve finished, the shows you loved or liked, and the shows you recommend to them — along with the day you last watched or rated each one, and how many episodes you’ve watched, in all and recently. They never see your email address, your phone number, your watch list, or the shows you didn’t like. You can hide a single show, pause sharing entirely, or remove a friend at any time. Your invite link, and any show you recommend from Screen Night, lets whoever opens it connect with you straight away, so share them with people you know. If one ends up somewhere you didn’t mean it to, choose Replace Invite Link in Friends Settings: old links stop working, and friends stay friends.

What you share remains yours. You give us permission to store it and show it to the friends you’ve connected with, only so that Friends can work, and that permission ends when you stop sharing or delete your account.
